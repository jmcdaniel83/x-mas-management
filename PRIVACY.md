# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 19, 2026_

**x-mas-management** is a personal tool used only by its author to manage their own Pinterest boards. It is not offered to or used by anyone else.

## What the app accesses

With the account owner's authorization, the app reads and updates the account owner's own Pinterest boards. Specifically, it lists boards, creates a new board, and changes an existing board's privacy setting.

## What the app stores

The app stores an OAuth access token and refresh token locally on the author's own computer so that it can call the Pinterest API. No Pinterest data is stored beyond what is needed to run a single command.

## What the app shares

The app does not collect, sell, share, or send any data to third parties. It does not use analytics, tracking, or advertising.

## Other users

The app does not access other Pinterest users' Pins, boards, or personal information.

## Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised as an issue on this repository: https://github.com/jmcdaniel83/x-mas-management/issues
